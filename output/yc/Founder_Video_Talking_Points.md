# Screenmate founder video talking points

Record about one minute with only the founder or founders speaking on camera. YC asks for all founders to appear, and for spontaneous speech from bullet points rather than a recited script. If founders are apart, a recorded video call is acceptable under the published guidance.

## One minute outline

- **0 to 7 seconds:** Introduce yourself as Viren Chauhan, building Screenmate. Include any other founders if applicable.
- **7 to 18 seconds:** Explain that it helps job seekers fill applications using their saved background, directly on employer websites.
- **18 to 33 seconds:** State the result: third globally out of 687 projects at AI Tinkerers' Agents Everywhere Hackathon with OpenAI. You represented Atlanta, the only U.S. chapter among the top three. Drop the geography sentence if time is tight.
- **33 to 48 seconds:** Explain that the prototype fills saved facts, drafts role-specific answers, and checks changes on the page. The applicant controls personal decisions and submission.
- **48 to 60 seconds:** Explain the next step: testing on the applications real job seekers use and measuring whether it saves time and earns repeat use.

The ready-to-practice wording is in `Founder_Video_Script.md`. The competition result establishes external recognition of the build; it is separate from customer usage or revenue.

## If there are multiple founders

Share the minute. Each person should say something substantive about their role or relevant background. Trim the product explanation to make room. Do not use a synthesized voice or product footage to stand in for a founder.

## Recording

Use a quiet room and face a window or soft light. Put the camera at eye level in landscape orientation. Leave a little space above your head. Speak to one person, not an auditorium. Keep the bullet points beside the camera and explain them in your own words.

Record two or three takes and choose the clearest. A small stumble is fine. Leave two seconds of quiet at the start and end. Upload the original file for trimming and light audio cleanup. No music, stock footage, slides, or elaborate transitions are needed.

Official instructions: https://www.ycombinator.com/video
