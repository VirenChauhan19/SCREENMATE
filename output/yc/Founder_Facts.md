# Founder facts needed to finish the application

Reply with short answers. Write none or zero only where accurate.

1. Founder name(s), role(s), email(s), and profile links you want to use.
2. Current city/country, current work or study, and full-time plans if accepted.
3. Solo founder or team; when and how founders met; whether all have met in person.
4. Start date, time commitment, and technical contributions for each founder. Mention contractors and AI tools accurately.
5. The real experience that led to Screenmate, and why you understand this problem.
6. One specific achievement per founder, preferably with a result or link.
7. Launch status; external users; recent active users; repeat users; paying users; revenue by month; and measurement dates.
8. Real feedback or observed user behavior. Indicate whether quoted feedback may be shared.
9. Pricing or intended business model, target customer, actual acquisition channels, and any measured costs.
10. Legal entity, ownership or planned split, investment, cash/spend if requested, and fundraising status.
11. Prior YC applications, accelerator participation, other ideas, and how you heard about YC.
12. Website URL you want to share, reviewer access instructions, and final video links when available.

The demo profile in the code is sample data. It has not been used to infer your legal name, education, location, or employment history.
