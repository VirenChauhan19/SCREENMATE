# Screenmate founder video practice script

About one minute at a conversational pace. Practice with this wording, then speak naturally from the talking points. If there are other founders, include them and share the speaking time.

Hi, I'm Viren Chauhan, and I'm building Screenmate.

Screenmate helps job seekers complete applications directly on employer websites, using the background they've already saved.

I built the prototype for AI Tinkerers' Agents Everywhere Hackathon with OpenAI. It placed third globally out of 687 submitted projects. I represented Atlanta, the only U.S. chapter among the top three.

The extension fills saved details, drafts answers for the role, and checks whether its changes reached the page. Personal decisions and final submission stay with the applicant.

The next step is testing it with job seekers on the applications they actually use, measuring where it saves time and what makes people come back.

The goal is to take the repeated work out of applying while keeping people in control of their answers.
