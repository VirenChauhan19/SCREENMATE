Hi, I'm Viren Chauhan and I'm building Screenmate. Applying for jobs means entering the same information into different websites, then writing answers for each role. Screenmate helps to do that directly on the application page. I built the prototype at the AI Tinkerers Agents Everywhere Hackathon with OpenAI. It placed third globally out of 687 projects. I represented Atlanta the only U.S. chapter among the top three. Screenmate uses your saved background to fill forms and draft answers. It checks that changes actually went through while leaving personal decisions and final submissions to you. The next step is testing it with job seekers on real applications, measuring where it saves time, fixing what breaks and understanding what makes people come back. I want to make applying less repetitive while keeping people in control of what they send.
