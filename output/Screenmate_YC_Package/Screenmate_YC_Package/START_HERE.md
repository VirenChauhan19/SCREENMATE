# Screenmate YC submission package

Prepared November 2, 2026 for the Winter 2027 application.

The on-time deadline listed by YC is November 2, 2026 at 8 p.m. Pacific / 11 p.m. Eastern. YC still considers late applications. Check https://www.ycombinator.com/apply before submitting.

## What to use

- **Screenmate_YC_Workbook.pdf**: application answers, founder fact sheet, recording instructions, and submission checklist in one readable document.
- **Application_Answers.md**: editable answers to paste into the online form. Replace every `[FOUNDER INPUT ...]` entry and confirm all proposed plans before pasting.
- **Screenmate_Overview.pdf**: optional one-page product summary. It is supporting material, not a verified application requirement.
- **Founder_Video_Talking_Points.md**: an outline for the required one-minute founder video. Record yourself on camera. All founders should speak if there is more than one.
- **Demo_Narration.md**: the product walkthrough narration to record in your own voice.
- **video/Screenmate_Product_Demo_Picture_Edit.mp4**: edited product footage ready for your voice recording. This is a picture edit with no voice track yet.
- **Evidence_and_Sources.md**: source links, claim boundaries, and the distinction between the recorded prototype and current code.

## Finish in this order

1. Supply the founder and business facts requested in `Founder_Facts.md`. Do not infer zero revenue, solo-founder status, incorporation, or ownership from missing information.
2. Review the product answers and decide whether the proposed customer segment, pricing experiment, distribution approach, and longer-term direction match your intentions.
3. Record the founder video using the bullet points. Speak naturally; do not read a script word for word.
4. Record the product narration. A phone voice memo is fine. Upload the original audio file here for cleanup and synchronization. The editor will fit the footage to your speech.
5. Upload the two completed videos separately using the method accepted by the current application. If links are requested, make them viewable without a login; unlisted is appropriate where supported. Test in a signed-out browser.
6. Open https://apply.ycombinator.com and paste the answers into the matching fields. The current form controls wording, branches, limits, and upload requirements. This package does not reproduce a verified signed-in form.
7. Complete founder profiles, business facts, video links, and any conditional questions. Search for `FOUNDER INPUT`, `PROPOSED`, and brackets before submitting.
8. Submit the application yourself and save the confirmation. Nothing in this package has been uploaded or submitted to YC.

## What is still needed

Your founder and business facts, your on-camera founder recording, your product narration, and final accessible video/product links. The demo uses existing footage from a controlled sample form; it is not evidence of a live employer submission or a current compatibility benchmark.

The application can be prepared without a pitch deck. These public instructions establish an online application and separate founder/demo video slots; the signed-in form is the authority on any other required attachments.
