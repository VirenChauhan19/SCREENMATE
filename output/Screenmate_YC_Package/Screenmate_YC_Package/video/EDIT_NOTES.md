`Screenmate_Product_Demo_Picture_Edit.mp4` is a 100-second, 1920 by 1080 picture edit at 24 frames per second. It has no audio track yet. Use it to review the visuals; the final narrated export will follow your voice recording.

The source is the existing repository recording `demo/screenmate-demo.mp4`. It shows a real execution of the extension on a controlled sample application. The edit replaces the older explanatory overlays, shortens waiting periods, and holds some footage longer for explanation. It does not represent a new live-site test or a real-time performance measurement. The sample-form disclosure stays visible during the walkthrough.

After your original recording arrives, the final pass will trim mistakes and excess pauses, apply gentle noise reduction and level balancing, synchronize the footage, and create captions from the actual spoken words. Your voice and phrasing will be preserved.
