Welcome to SCREENMATE

SCREENMATE fills job applications from your profile, asks you what only you can answer, and submits only when you confirm. Two minutes of setup:
  1. Add your resume below. It fills your profile and gets attached to applications for you.
  2. Check your profile, and add a phone number: nearly every form asks for one.
  3. Answer the standing questions once, so you are not asked on every site.
  4. Open any job application and click the SCREENMATE pill, or press Alt+Shift+S.

Your resume

Saved on this device. SCREENMATE attaches it where an application asks for a resume, and reads it to fill your profile.

Your profile

The only source of facts about you. The agent cannot write a value that is not here.
Used to ground answers about your past work. Keep details accurate; SCREENMATE will not invent missing experience.

Standing answers

Save answers to common questions. SCREENMATE reuses matching answers and leaves unknown optional questions blank. Choose Ask each time when you want to decide on every application.

Remembered answers

Answers you gave on an application and asked SCREENMATE to remember. Delete one to be asked again.

Backend

Where the agent runs. OpenRouter and Exa keys live on that server, never in the extension.
